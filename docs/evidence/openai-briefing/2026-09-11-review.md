All candidate numeric temperatures, probabilities, and broad weather timing matched the
facts. That alone is insufficient: the thunderstorm candidate concluded:

> Carry an umbrella or seek shelter if storms develop this evening.

Offering those as alternatives is unsuitable. [National Weather Service guidance](https://www.weather.gov/safety/lightning-safety-overview)
calls for safe shelter when thunderstorms threaten, identifying substantial buildings and
hard-topped vehicles as safe options. A future revision should omit umbrella advice for
thunderstorms and use one appropriate conditional takeaway. The automatic validator
accepted this output, demonstrating a gap beyond numeric/coverage validation.

The low-chance candidate also enumerated this afternoon, this evening, overnight,
tomorrow morning, and tomorrow afternoon. A future revision should give a single summary
of consistently low chances without supplying an all-daypart peak list for prose to copy.
